BATTLESHIP on-chain · testnet v 13:32 …

A two-player game where a hit is called by the Kaspa network, not by our server. Your board is frozen cryptographically and never leaves this browser — every "hit or miss" has to be proven with a Merkle path, which the network recomputes and rejects if it doesn't add up. Lying about a hit is impossible. Every move is a real testnet transaction signed with your key; you need no wallet, we cover the fees.

Why you wait for your opponent to confirm

Because only they know what your shot hit. Their board never leaves their browser — neither our server nor the chain has a copy. The fact "you hit C3" exists nowhere until they produce it and prove it. If anyone could tell you sooner, that someone would know their layout — and the game would be pointless.

It works exactly like a real table. You call "C3", then wait for your opponent to say "miss". If they stepped out for a minute, you wait until they're back. The one difference is that here they cannot lie: every hit or miss must be proven, and the Kaspa network checks the proof.

That is why their board shows whether they are at the table (answers instantly) or away (answers when back). And if they never come back, the game ends in your favour once the clock runs out — automatically, even with your browser closed.

What the network enforces — and what it does not, yet

Five rules are enforced by consensus: breaking any of them means the transaction is rejected by nodes, not by us. Truthful hit-or-miss answers (Merkle proof), turn order, no shooting the same square twice, a full fleet on the opponent's board (freeze an empty board and after the eighteenth miss you can no longer move, so you lose), and a monotonic game clock.

Two things stay off-chain. Your browser signs a transaction built by our server without inspecting its contents — a malicious server could swap the move. And the network does not verify the shape of the fleet: seven separate single-square ships pass just like a legal layout. That is why this runs on testnet only, with nothing at stake.
